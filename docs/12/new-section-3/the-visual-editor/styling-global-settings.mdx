Make your documentation truly yours. Using the Visual Editor, you can customize the visual theme of your site in real-time, as well as configure essential global settings like custom domains, access control, and analytics.

## Customizing Your Theme

The Theme Settings panel allows you to adjust the visual identity of your documentation to match your brand. As you make changes, the preview frame updates instantly so you can see exactly how your site will look before going live.

You have full control over:
*   **Primary Color:** Set the main accent color used for buttons, links, and active states.
*   **Typography:** Choose a font family (like *Inter*) that aligns with your brand guidelines.
*   **Dark Mode:** Enable or disable dark mode support for your readers.

### Applying a Theme Preset

If you want a great-looking site quickly, you can use one of our built-in theme presets.

<Steps>
<Step title="Open Theme Settings">
While in the Visual Editor, locate and open the **Theme Settings** panel on the side of your screen.
</Step>
<Step title="Choose a Preset">
Click the **Presets** dropdown to select a pre-configured design. You can choose from styles like **Minimal**, **Corporate**, or **Playful**.
</Step>
<Step title="Fine-tune your design">
Once a preset is applied, you can still manually adjust the primary color, font, or dark mode settings to perfect the look.
</Step>
</Steps>

> [!TIP]
> Changes in the Theme Settings panel update in real-time in your editor. Feel free to experiment—your readers won't see the changes until you hit publish!

## Managing Global Settings

Global settings control the behind-the-scenes behavior of your documentation, such as where it is hosted and who has permission to view it. 

To access these options, click the **⚙️ Settings** button in the Visual Editor toolbar. This opens the global settings dashboard.

| Setting Category | What you can do |
|------------------|-----------------|
| **🌐 Domain** | Set up a custom domain (e.g., `docs.example.com`) and check the status of your automatic SSL certificate. |
| **🔒 Access** | Toggle your documentation audience between **Public** and **Private**. For private docs, configure authentication methods like GitDocAI login or SAML SSO. |
| **👥 Team** | View your current workspace members and manage permissions for Editors and Readers. |
| **📊 Analytics** | Track your documentation's performance, see a quick snapshot of monthly page views, and click through to view full traffic reports. |

> [!NOTE]
> When you configure a custom domain, GitDocAI automatically provisions and manages a free SSL certificate for you. It may take a few minutes for the "Active" status to appear after updating your DNS records.
